From Clinical Frustration to Technical Innovation

Two decades at the bedside — critical care, neonatology, infectious disease, and now clinical research operations. Different units, different specialties, same pattern every time: clinicians working against their own tools, every single shift.

Every system I build comes from a real problem I've lived. I don't design from a spec sheet — I design from the bedside, where a bad interface costs minutes you don't have.

My approach: identify the broken workflow, design the fix, build a working prototype, and prove it works. No committee. No 18-month sprint. Some of it is single-file HTML you carry in on a thumb drive; some of it is packaged software with a test suite and a manual. The through-line is the same — a real problem, fixed end to end.

STUDY//SCOUT — Clinical-Trials Business Development Terminal

Active Development

Packaged terminal application for research-site business development. Hunts the ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API, then regroups every result into one dossier per sponsor — their whole book of trials, contacts deduped across NCT numbers, pipeline history and notes in a single view. Kanban pipeline board, morning briefing of what changed overnight, follow-up clocks, and an AI consigliere that drafts consolidated outreach and researches sponsors on the live web. Ships as a real .rpm/.deb with a test suite, a self-diagnostic doctor, owner-only backups of the pipeline, and a manual.

KEY:Site BD is relationship work, but every tool on the market treats it as listing-search. This one changes the unit of work from the study to the sponsor — and deliberately refuses to compute a fit score, because that judgment belongs to the person making the call.

NEC Tool — Perez-Serize NEC Predictive Tool

Research / Validation

Dual-component clinical prediction engine for necrotizing enterocolitis. Monitors 9 biomarkers (ANC, WBC, platelets, CRP, fecal calprotectin, pH, lactate, base deficit, eosinophils), scoring both absolute abnormality and temporal trajectory to detect NEC 24-48 hours before clinical deterioration. Evidence-based thresholds from published literature with clinical pattern bonuses for multi-system co-occurring abnormalities. 5,900+ lines with animated demo walkthrough, algorithm explainer, and validation dashboard.

KEY:No single biomarker diagnoses NEC. This tool combines 9 markers across time — trajectory analysis that creates a synergistic risk assessment where three high-priority markers dropping simultaneously is more predictive than any marker alone.

Development Approach

☤

Clinical First

Every feature starts with understanding the clinical need, not the technical possibility.

⚡

Rapid Iteration

Build, test with actual clinicians, refine. No solution survives first contact with a busy unit.

⚙

Bulletproof

If it doesn't work at 3 AM with stressed staff, it doesn't work.

⚖

Model Proposes, Code Verifies

Any AI in these tools is on a leash: eligibility logic, dosing windows and scoring are ordinary auditable code, never a generated guess. A model may draft the email — it never decides who qualifies.

⚙

Zero Friction

No servers. No logins. No IT approval. Open the file and it works.
